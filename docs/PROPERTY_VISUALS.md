# Demo residence and consistent layout views

The fictional demo home is 2,000 sq ft over two floors on a 40 × 25 ft footprint, built in 2000. These are user-specified fixture facts, not retrieved records. Blender reads the same SiteContext for both render profiles. Uploaded photos are unchanged. Legacy single-story AI references are excluded from active matching.

The initial property image is a labeled AI visualization of a bare lawn, tree, fence and small patio. Its matching property model remains available under Blender model. The three concept cards are appearance references. After selection, Concept and Blender use one deterministic current scene, keyed to ProjectSpec geometry. AI appearance studies are separately labeled because their object placement can drift. Edits invalidate the displayed scene; old saved demos refresh residence context without moving their selected features.

The two 4 ft deep door landing zones are preliminary fixture assumptions. Blocking objects raise a door-clearance conflict; walkable surfaces are permitted. Actual egress, circulation and grill operating clearances need on-site verification.

Assets created with the built-in image-generation tool: `public/demo/photoreal/property-before-two-story.png`, `meadow-simple-two-story.png`, `terrace-kitchen-two-story.png`, `retreat-pool-two-story.png` (same folder). Blender assets: `public/demo/property-max.png` and `public/demo/{meadow,terrace,retreat}-layout.png`. Reproduce geometry renders with `npm run render:property` and `node --import tsx scripts/render-tier-scenes.ts`.
